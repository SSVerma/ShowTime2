✨ Everything You Need

Engineered for Cinephiles & Film Lovers

ShowTime 2.0 combines lightning-fast movie exploration with personal cinema journaling, community curation, and aesthetic social sharing.

🎬

Explore & Discover

Browse trending, top-rated, and upcoming releases. View cast filmographies, trailers, seasons, episodes, and streaming availability powered by JustWatch.

📖

Cinema Diary & Journal

Log watch dates, rewatch counts, half-star ratings, private reviews, and custom tags. Track your cinephile journey with rich viewing statistics.

🍿

Curated Community Lists

Curate themed collections, share them with the cinephile community, clone lists created by fellow movie lovers, and vote in daily community polls.

🧾

Vintage Cinema Receipts

Generate aesthetic retro thermal receipts for your watch history, favorites, or custom lists to share directly on Instagram and Twitter.

🎮

Daily Cinema Challenge

Test your film knowledge with daily guess-the-movie trivia puzzles, track your guessing streaks, and share spoiler-free score grids.

⚡

Smart Multi-Facet Search

Instant search across millions of titles, actors, and directors. Filter in real-time by streaming platform, release year, genres, and ratings.

☁️

Trakt & Cloud Backup

Automatic two-way scrobbling with Trakt.tv and encrypted Google Cloud Firestore snapshots to safeguard your entire movie library across devices.

📅

Calendar Sync & Alerts

Export upcoming release dates directly to your device calendar (.ics) with one tap, and get alerts when new episodes and movie premieres air.

🎨

Material You & OLED Dark

Dynamic wallpaper theming adapting to your color palette, high-frame-rate Compose animations, and a pure pitch-black AMOLED Midnight mode.

🔒

Privacy-First & Open Source

100% offline-first local SQLite Room database. No data selling, transparent Apache-2.0 code, and completely free of invasive popups.
